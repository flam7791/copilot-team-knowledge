=== TRM-0001 | Term | Golden dataset ===
Status: active | Classification: internal | Verified: 2026-05-11 by Analytics lead | Review by: 2027-05-11
Owner: Analytics lead
Tags: data-quality; reporting

## Summary

In this team, a golden dataset is a table that has an owner, documented definitions and automated quality checks, and is the only approved source for the figures it holds. It does not mean "the newest" or "the largest" copy of the data.

## Details

- Golden datasets are marked with the data-quality tag in the catalogue.

- Team definition agreed in May 2026.
