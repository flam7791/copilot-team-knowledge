=== DEC-0002 | Decision | Review cloud costs every month ===
Status: active | Classification: internal | Verified: 2026-02-05 by Team lead | Review by: 2027-02-05
Owner: Data platform lead
Tags: cost; governance

## Summary

The team reviews cloud spend in the first team meeting of each month, against the baseline in FCT-0001. Any service whose monthly cost rises by more than 20% is investigated by its owner before the next review.

## Details

- Owner of the review: the data platform lead.
- Input: the finance cost export for the previous month.
- Output: one line per investigated service in the meeting notes.

## Evidence

- Agreed at the 3 February 2026 team meeting; the 20% threshold is stated in the notes.

=== DEC-0003 | Decision | Refresh reporting dashboards every four hours ===
Status: active | Classification: internal | Verified: 2026-03-14 by Data platform lead | Review by: 2027-03-14
Owner: Data platform lead
Tags: dashboards; reporting; data-platform

## Summary

Since 16 March 2026, reporting dashboards refresh every four hours (00:00, 04:00, 08:00, 12:00, 16:00 and 20:00 UTC). Business users asked for same-day figures, and the move to a usage-based warehouse plan removed the reason for a single nightly load.

## Details

- Replaces the nightly refresh (DEC-0001).
- Refreshes that fail are retried once after 30 minutes; a second failure alerts the data platform lead.

## Evidence

- Decided at the 12 March 2026 architecture review (MTG-0001).
- The cost impact was estimated in the same meeting at under 5% of monthly spend; this is an estimate, not a measured figure.
