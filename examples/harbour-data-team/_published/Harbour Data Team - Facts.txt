=== FCT-0001 | Fact | Monthly cloud spend baseline ===
Status: active | Classification: internal | Verified: 2026-07-08 by Data platform lead | Review by: 2027-01-08

Tags: cost

## Summary

The team's average monthly cloud spend was EUR 18,400 over January to June 2026. This is the baseline for the monthly cost review (DEC-0002).

## Details

- Covers the warehouse, storage and the reporting service; excludes software licences.

=== FCT-0002 | Fact | Retention period for raw event logs ===
Status: active | Classification: internal | Verified: 2026-04-20 by Team lead | Review by: 2027-04-20
Owner: Data platform lead
Tags: retention; governance

## Summary

Raw event logs are kept for 13 months and then deleted automatically. Aggregated tables built from them are kept for as long as the reports that use them.

## Details

- 13 months allows a full year-on-year comparison with one month's margin.

## Evidence

- Section 3 of the data retention schedule, version 2 (2026).

Sources:
- Data retention schedule, version 2, 2026 - https://contoso.sharepoint.com/sites/harbour-data/Shared%20Documents/Policies/Data%20retention%20schedule%20v2.docx
