## Summary

Ask for access through the service desk form "Reporting workspace access", naming the business unit and the reports needed. Your line manager approves; access is usually granted within two working days.

## Details

1. Open the service desk form "Reporting workspace access".
2. Choose the business unit and list the reports you need.
3. Your line manager receives the approval request.
4. Once approved, the data platform lead grants read access.
