## Summary

The team reviews cloud spend in the first team meeting of each month, against the baseline in FCT-0001. Any service whose monthly cost rises by more than 20% is investigated by its owner before the next review.

## Details

- Owner of the review: the data platform lead.
- Input: the finance cost export for the previous month.
- Output: one line per investigated service in the meeting notes.

## Evidence

- Agreed at the 3 February 2026 team meeting; the 20% threshold is stated in the notes.
