## Summary

Raw event logs are kept for 13 months and then deleted automatically. Aggregated tables built from them are kept for as long as the reports that use them.

## Details

- 13 months allows a full year-on-year comparison with one month's margin.

- Section 3 of the data retention schedule, version 2 (2026).
