CURATE - propose draft cards from a final source (Microsoft 365 Copilot Chat)

Use after something is final: approved minutes, a decision note that was sent, a published report, a closed project update. Never curate from drafts.

EACH SOURCE
1. Open a new chat in Microsoft 365 Copilot Chat, with Work selected.
2. Type "/" and add the catalogue bundle ("<Team> - 00 Catalogue.txt") and the final source document.
3. Paste the prompt below, paste your tag list (the "tags" in kb.yaml) where indicated, and send.
4. For each proposed card, the curator runs "teamkb new <type> <title>" to get the real ID and file, copies the proposed fields and text into it, and checks every statement and figure against the source.
5. The owner of the card verifies it: sets status to active and fills "verified" and "review_by". Then run "teamkb validate", "teamkb index" and "teamkb bundle".
